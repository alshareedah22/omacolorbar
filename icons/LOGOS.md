# Distro logos

`distro-*.svg` are made by `tools/make_distro_logos.py` from Simple Icons 16.36.0
(https://simpleicons.org, CC0 1.0), each path unaltered in the brand color Simple Icons
lists. The logos are trademarks of their projects; OmaColorBar shows them only so you
can mark your bar with the distro you use, and implies no endorsement.

Some source drawings carry their own license, kept here as required:

- Debian logo: CC BY-SA 3.0, Software in the Public Interest, Inc.
- Gentoo logo: CC BY-SA 2.5, Gentoo Foundation.
- Rocky Linux logo: CC BY-SA 4.0, Rocky Enterprise Software Foundation.
- NixOS logo: CC BY 4.0, NixOS Foundation.
- Garuda Linux and MX Linux logos: GPL-3.0-only, as listed by Simple Icons.
- Fedora logo: used under the Fedora brand guidelines,
  https://docs.fedoraproject.org/en-US/project/brand/

AlmaLinux, Slackware, Nobara, MX Linux and SUSE are black or near black in Simple Icons; OmaColorBar draws them in the bar's
text color so they stay visible on a dark bar.

`menu-arch.svg` is the Arch Linux logo in its official `#1793D1`, under the Arch Linux
trademark policy. `menu-omarchy.svg` is traced from the Omarchy font's U+E900 glyph.
